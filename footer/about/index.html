Welcome to VUEduTech, a dedicated educational platform created to support Virtual University students in their academic journey. Founded by Sir Sharjeel Khalid, VUEduTech was established with a clear vision: to make quality educational resources easily accessible to every Virtual University student and help them achieve academic success with confidence.
Our platform provides a wide range of educational resources, including handout notes, past papers, quizzes, assignments guidance, midterm preparation materials, final term preparation resources, study tips, academic updates, and much more. Every resource is designed to help students learn effectively and perform better in their academic programs.

About the Founder – Sir Sharjeel Khalid

VUEduTech was founded by Sir Sharjeel Khalid, an educator, content creator, and technology enthusiast who is passionate about helping students succeed. Through years of experience working with Virtual University students, he recognized the need for a reliable platform that could simplify learning and provide students with easy access to educational content.
His mission is not only to provide study materials but also to guide students throughout their academic journey. By combining education and technology, Sir Sharjeel Khalid has built VUEduTech into a valuable learning platform that supports thousands of students seeking academic excellence.
His dedication to student success extends beyond the website through educational content, tutorials, exam preparation videos, and guidance sessions designed specifically for Virtual University learners.

VU Updates Tech – Educational Content Beyond the Website

In addition to VUEduTech, Sir Sharjeel Khalid is also the creator of VU Updates Tech, a growing educational YouTube channel dedicated to Virtual University students.
VU Updates Tech serves as an extension of the learning experience provided by VUEduTech. Through the channel, students can access:

  • Midterm and Final Term Preparation Videos
  • Important and Repeated MCQs
  • Assignment Guidance
  • Quiz Preparation
  • Study Tips and Strategies

  • Virtual University Updates

The goal of VU Updates Tech is to ensure that students receive both written and video-based learning support. Whether students prefer reading study notes or watching detailed explanations, they can find the resources they need through VUEduTech and VU Updates Tech.
